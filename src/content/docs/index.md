Documentation for every connected service repository appears under **Services**.
Pages are drafted by the sync pipeline, checked against their source by an LLM judge,
and published either automatically (trusted repos) or after a technical writer approves a pull request.

See the [README](https://github.com/your-org/ai-multisync-tool#readme) for setup.
